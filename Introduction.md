 FinOps-AgentOS is a smart AI traffic controller.

It will:
1. 🧠 Choose the right AI model — local Ollama or cloud AI, depending on the task.
2. 💰 Control costs — avoid expensive AI calls when cheaper options are suitable.
3. 📊 Monitor performance — track tokens, costs, speed and failures.
4. 🛡️ Protect sensitive data — prevent unauthorized cloud access.
5. 🤖 Eventually automate optimization — suggest cheaper infrastructure or model configurations, with human approval.
Example with Stormscope:

A user asks about dangerous weather → FinOps-AgentOS chooses an approved AI model to explain the risk → tracks its cost and performance.

Current status: The first version provides basic routing, illustrative cost estimates and monitoring. Intelligent optimization and infrastructure automation are future steps.

Ultimate goal: Run enterprise AI systems reliably, securely and at the lowest justified cost.
